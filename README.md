## Selected work here

- **[lane-queue](https://github.com/juliojm13/lane-queue)** — the Lane Queue pattern: ~100 dependency-free
  lines that stop `asyncio` race conditions from corrupting an AI agent's context window. Basis of my 2026 talk.
- **[utpl-chatbot](https://github.com/juliojm13/utpl-chatbot)** — FastAPI + LangChain (Gemini) Telegram
  chatbot in a hexagonal architecture, with PostgreSQL, Alembic migrations and tests.
- **[atrucks](https://github.com/juliojm13/atrucks)** — Dockerized Django service that resolves a phone
  number to its carrier and region.
- **[tools_demo](https://github.com/juliojm13/tools_demo)** — minimal LangChain tool-calling example from my
  FLISoL talk.
